# Code of conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Help newcomers, discuss work constructively, and welcome different experiences and communication styles.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, doxxing and spam are unacceptable in project spaces.

Raise concerns with Michael Tolson through a contact route on [his profile](https://github.com/Mtolson415). If no suitable private route is available, request one in an issue without names or sensitive details. Do not publish private evidence.

The maintainer may remove harmful contributions, warn participants or restrict participation according to severity/repetition. Reports are handled as privately as practical. This policy also applies to the maintainer.
